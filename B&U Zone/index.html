Our Story: Built in Lahore with Passion

Hi there! We are Bilal and Ubaid. Like most mobile users in Pakistan, we were sick and tired of cheap cables that snapped after two weeks, chargers that heated up dangerously, and counterfeit earbuds that sounded like a tin can.

In 2023, right from the bustling tech hub of Hafeez Centre & Gulberg, Lahore, we founded B&U Zone with a singular mission:

"Bring durable, high-performance, and stylish mobile accessories to everyday consumers at prices that don't hurt their wallet, backed by real customer care."

Every single product you see on this website is personally tested by us on multiple iPhones, Samsungs, and Android flagships. We test charging speeds, heat dissipation, and drop endurance so that you can order with 100% peace of mind.
